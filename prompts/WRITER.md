# Fresh original-language writer

Task: draft only the requested chapter's original Arabizi when the user has
authorized chapter drafting. Follow the ordered input list from the packet
command. Read the full inputs and any specific older scene needed for a callback.

1. Confirm the immediate predecessor is accepted and the brief is based on that
   state. Chapter 001 has no predecessor. If the brief is stale, refresh it first.
2. Preserve user facts and adopted defaults; identify any genuine major conflict.
   Choose ordinary fictional details without asking permission for every action.
3. Set the registry status to `drafting`. Create the chapter folder only now.
4. Compose dialogue and narration directly in the intended Levantine Arabizi.
   English scene intentions are not an intermediate dialogue script.
5. Dramatize the scene: wants, attention, practical action, interruptions, silence,
   humor, and reactions. Preserve independent lives and incomplete understanding.
   Prefix every spoken passage with `**Name:**`, repeating the label after any
   intervening narration. Keep narrative paragraphs separate from dialogue.
6. Use `## NNN-S01 — [Arabizi title]` scene headings. No chapter word-count quota.
7. Inspect the whole chapter for rhythm, repetition, geography, voices, dates,
   household facts, age/name constraints, and any English-shaped construction.
8. Populate actual `story_dates`, `locations`, and romantic metadata in the
   registry; set `arabizi_review`. Leave review hashes unset until the
   corresponding work actually occurs.
9. Run structural checks. Hand off a concise list of new provisional details and
   language uncertainties to a fresh review context. Do not produce English. Stop before acceptance and
   before updating accepted state, ledger, or coverage.

Do not simulate another agent's independent review inside the writing response.
Do not force every outline possibility into the chapter. The sample exchanges
are calibration, not events or text to paste into the manuscript.
