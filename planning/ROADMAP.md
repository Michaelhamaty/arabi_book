# Book roadmap (S011, revised by S017 on 2026-11-11)

Everything after the last settled chapter is a plan, not a story event. The
planner refreshes the next brief from what actually happened and updates this
file when the settled chapters suggest a better path. Weekdays and church
dates below are computed for 2027; verify other public dates before using them.

## Shape and length

- About **36 chapters of 1,800–3,000 words** (roughly 85–95k words).
  Barcelona I: 002–019. Lebanon: 020–027. Barcelona II: 028–036.
- **Central question:** Michael says yes to everyone. Over 2027 he learns what
  it costs to keep every door open, and chooses fewer, deeper commitments
  without becoming smaller or colder.
- **Engines:** the overcommitment habit (often rewarded before it is
  punished); Yara; Walid versus Marwan over what the café is; paid work with
  Nadine; friendships that need maintenance (Rami, Inés, Karim, Sami);
  family at a distance; Lebanon as a dream versus a place.
- **Flirtation (C019):** flirting and small physical intimacy belong in all
  three parts, not only with Yara: Barcelona I (Dalia, Salma, others), Lebanon,
  and Barcelona II, where the same Barcelona people pick up where they left
  off. Everyone 20+, ages explicit; no exclusivity unless agreed on the page.
- **Rules of motion:** every chapter changes something. Skip dull time. Let
  consequences arrive late. Not every thread appears in every chapter. Give
  the book pleasure: beaches, festivals, food, flirtation, argument, faith.

## Working ending

New Year's Eve, **Friday, December 31, 2027**, at 3al Zawiye. The café
survives in some changed form. Abu Nabil and Michael play the rematch that has
run all year. Yara is there, and what they are to each other is honest and
hopeful rather than tied up. Michael's phone holds the people he has kept, not
everyone he said yes to. A call home closes the book. Revisable.

## Part 3 — Lebanon, July 16 to September 15 (020–027)

**Tarek (26, Lebanese civil engineer, Rami's old friend)** is weighing a job
abroad while Michael dreams of staying. Beaches, mountains, waterfalls, Beirut
nights, power cuts, salaries, hospitality that is also work for the host.
Yara is expected in Beirut in August with her family (013); she fears being
the guide through whom Michael loves Beirut. Both there, nothing scheduled.
Barcelona's news (the café fight, Rami) continues by phone. Settled 019
shapes the summer: Yara's August has terms said aloud (no promise either
side; she is not his guide; if he finds a Beirut sea with no doorman, not
Ramlet el Baida, she comes; no `mesh hayda el so2al`; anything that happens
she hears from him, not Camille); she visits the Mar Mi5a2il office in
August and tells her mother herself. Michael does no work for Nadine in the
summer (he answers her calls). Marwan's ultimatum is Walid's to think
over. The "maybe I live here" dream meets ordinary reality without becoming
bleak. Flirtation in Lebanon too (C019): someone new, adult and
with her own life, alongside whatever Yara and Michael are in August. Do not invent Michael's real
relatives or family history in Lebanon.

Settled 020: Michael lodges in Wassim's room in Tarek's Badaro flat (master
§15). Tarek's fictional Riyadh offer awaits his answer by **Sat Jul 31**;
only Michael knows, sworn to silence, Rami included. Tarek invited him to
the Chouf on Sun Jul 18 and promised Jbeil on Sat Jul 24. Nayla (25) is
on the page; the flirtation is hers to shape. Possible later Lebanon nodes
(plans): the Sunday at Baakline where hospitality is the family's work;
Tarek's Jul 31 answer (Rami told by Tarek, not Michael); Yara's August and
the sea with no doorman; Nayla on her own terms (anything with her, Yara
hears from Michael). Next flirtations find a physical language other than
hands (017, 019, 020).
