## Intent

Three weeks on, nobody has apologized, and people have quietly stopped
telling Michael things. On the day of books and roses Rami's life moves
without him, Inés asks him for something, and Yara, who has heard, raises
the question they've dodged since Barceloneta. Joy first: the busiest dawn
of his working life, a street of strangers, a rose.

## Bridge — April in a cold flat

Days, not feelings: Rami cooks for two; the blue tub is full again; the
3:00 alarm keeps Michael out of the evenings; Inés's prints dry over the
corner she gave him; Rami's euro stays where Michael put it. **Michael
acts:** each morning he leaves the forn's crooked loaves on the table,
wrapped, no note: the staff's share, scored by his own hands, so by Rami's
code his to give. Something eats them. He never learns who. Yara keeps her
jury news "for face to face".

## Scenes

**S01 — Pilar's, Sant Jordi dawn.** The year's busiest morning: pa de Sant
Jordi (cheese and sobrassada stripes), a queue from seven, Arnau trusted
with the festive bread, Roser and Jaume with their own Sant Jordi. Michael
loves it and can't taste a crumb (one beat). The turn: Inés's first message
in three weeks asks him for something. A rose reaches his hands; Pilar
knows whom it's for.

**S02 — Gràcia, afternoon.** On his name day Jordi has given Inés the corner
of his Catalan class's book stall (fictional). Rami set her part up that
morning: a line, kitchen pegs, prices in his marker. Her want: to see if
pictures nobody commissioned sell under her name in letters bigger than an
ant. Glossy prints; her mother's photo hangs there unpriced. Rami is leaving
for Quim's as Michael arrives. Answering Jordi's small talk, Rami states the
fact of his life: he told Quim **yes** to the grill, full-time from May.
Inés already knew. Michael hears it as a bystander and says nothing. If it
fits, Rami has also taken his mother's sauce off the menu. Then Inés's ask:
she can invoice, but she can't talk a stranger into a picture. His gift,
invited, and he loves it. He leaves her prices and layout alone, though he
has opinions. When someone asks for the mother photo, he hands the question
to Inés. What she decides is hers.

**S03 — Night: Yara and a rose.** The first real, two-sided talk about what
they are. Her news: the jury (more mixed than pass or fail); placements she
is applying for; family expecting her in Beirut in August. His, which she
never knew: Lebanon from July 16. Camille told her he passed on something
that wasn't his. She asks what; he won't say. Loyalty or another closed
door? Her doubts are her own: a thing with a July date, being one of his
yeses, a man who might love Beirut through her. Something is said that can't
be unsaid. A kiss is fine; no label. A stairwell on a timer light would suit
`Darraj fi jiran`. End on a kind of close the book hasn't used.

## What changes

Rami has answered Quim: yes, full-time from May; Michael heard it last, by
accident. Inés asked, and Michael gave without improving: that rift shifts
sideways, unforgiven; with Rami it sets into politeness. Michael and Yara
have said what they're unsure of, one thing that can't be taken back, and
each knows the other's summer.
