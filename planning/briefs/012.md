## Intent

The confidence Rami gave on the balcony breaks, and it breaks out of love.
Rami's alibi is Michael's own Easter, so when Inés finally asks what
`2 ayyar` means, she asks the owner of the date. In 010 carrying words opened
a door; carrying a confidence closes one. The cost is stranger than a
scolding: the two-year flatmates close around each other and Michael ends
outside. Joy first.

## Scenes

**S01 — Tuesday dawn, behind Pilar's glass.** Paid work he loves: the oven
at four, no talk in the dough, Arnau in the white apron, Roser's and
Jaume's follow-ups. Then Pilar asks Michael one, about something he said
yesterday: he has become a regular. Nobody here asks about the flat. At seven the
shutter rises on Yara. She is off an all-nighter, her own work in hand
(the stair maquette, a jury at nine; writer's choice). Pilar takes her
measure. A few minutes in the waking street: her life, teasing both ways,
nothing defined.

**S02 — Tuesday, late afternoon, the flat.** Inés is back. Sevilla is on the
table: frames the magazine took, her name to go under them, and one she
didn't send, her mother. She gives little away. She reads Rami by his
fridge (the m7ammara tub empty for the first time in two years), his phone
face down, his afternoon sleep, and fears he's leaving. Because `2 ayyar` is
Michael's Easter, she asks Michael. He reassures her and then tells all of
it: Quim's offer, the end of the degree, the mother who tells el Sha8our her
son "helps out" at night. He adds a plan for her to carry to Rami: his name
on the menu, a price for the sauce, part-time until he graduates. Inés isn't
relieved: two years, and she has eaten that sauce all along. She warned
Michael she keeps no secrets. Having just used a paid trip to go home
without admitting it, she understands Rami's alibi better than Michael does.
She promises nothing.

**Bridge (optional, ≤200 words) — Dalia's, Tuesday night.** Walid in the
blue shirt, Marwan. In Dalia's room each partner sees the
other's argument. Nothing is decided. Or pass it offstage in a line.

**S03 — Wednesday evening.** Inés told Rami that night. It was no slip: she
refuses to know something about him behind his back. Rami is due on Quim's
grill, so the fight has a clock. Make it specific: his pride at being a
project; the sauce Michael wanted to price; money, from a man who never
asked his own pay; the degree as the story el Sha8our hears; the Easter
Michael lent, then lent out (`Fes7i`). Rami's code, only what his own hands
made, can turn on Michael. Inés is caught between them, angry at both and at
herself, and won't take a side on cue. Michael's one attempt to explain
doesn't land. The turn: Rami takes his deadline back off Michael's
calendar (011: `Bterja3lak yeh 2 ayyar, ndif`). His answer to Quim stays
open. He goes to work. End on a new kind of ending, perhaps the flat that
night, heard from Michael's room, going on without him. Not on dialogue in transit, a bell, an action at a table, a closing door, or
a still object.

## What changes

Inés knows Rami's offer, and Rami knows who told her. The borrowed Easter has
been returned, and May 2 is no longer Rami's deadline by Michael's calendar.
Rami and Inés are on one side and Michael is outside, unmended into 013.
Michael is a regular at Pilar's, and he has seen Yara at work.
