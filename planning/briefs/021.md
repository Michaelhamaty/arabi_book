## Intent

Michael promised one silence and comes home from Baakline holding two. The
mother who counts a guest as three has noticed what her son hasn't said, and
she wants it from him, at her table, not from a guest or a phone. By Saturday
the truth moves without Michael saying a word.

## Scenes

**S01 — Baakline.** From the lane, the pound of the stone `jorn`: Em Tarek
has been making kebbe since five. Hospitality is labour: her arm, the tray,
the photo for `El 3ayle`, eating last and standing. Tarek, a host by reflex,
sits in the guest seat with nothing for his hands; she takes the tray from
him. Michael sees what `sert dayf 3and emme` means and, unlike 020, lets
himself be hosted: refusing would insult the work. Abu Tarek, who built the
house, may ask his engineer son one question about the rusting rebar left on
the roof for a second floor; one exchange, no speech. The turn: Em Tarek
takes Michael alone (the roof, the za3tar drying, or the fig tree) and asks,
as Tarek predicted, whether he ate, then about her son. He must finish
Tarek's sentence himself (`qella enno ana—`), with something true that is
not the secret. She has her own evidence (for example, Tarek asked for his
original degree certificate, as Wassim did before Dubai). She asks nothing
he can't refuse, then binds him: don't tell Tarek she asked. On the way
down, Tarek asks what she wanted; Michael's answer is true and small (she
asked whether he ate), and Tarek laughs.

**Bridge (short).** Em Tarek adds Michael to `El 3ayle`; thirty people now
send him voice notes and ask after Tarek. The week passes in Beirut's heat
with both silences held. A few paragraphs.

**S02 — Jbeil.** Tarek keeps the promise (`mesh 5yar`) after a Saturday
half-day: coast traffic, the dead car AC, the guide voice tried once and
dropped. The harbour is a place, not a view: fishermen, boys on the wall.
Rami calls Michael on his split-shift break, rough and hurt: Em Tarek has
phoned him three times this week, Tarek answers him with photos of kebbe,
and Rami knows Michael knows. Michael holds; Rami swears, then demands
Tarek. Michael hands over the phone and says nothing else. Tarek walks the
wall with it; Michael waits with the old fisherman (fish, bait, the boys).
Tarek comes back having told Rami himself, and having learned from Rami that
his mother called. He asks Michael whether she asked him anything.

**Ending.** A new type: a literally true answer that keeps Em Tarek's
confidence and still tells Tarek everything: Sunday's small answer, the same
words, now heard by a son who knows she called Rami. Stop there or one beat
later; narration doesn't explain.
None of the spent endings (S019 list), no water, no still object.

## What changes

Rami knows, from Tarek. Em Tarek has reached for her son through Rami, and
Tarek knows she knows something. Michael kept both silences and they held,
yet the truth travelled through the people themselves. Rami's anger at
Michael for knowing stays open. Tarek's answer is still his, due Jul 31.
