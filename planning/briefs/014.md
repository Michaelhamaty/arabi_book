## Intent

Five weeks after the city's Easter, Michael gets his own, and it is
pleasure: candles, a parish with no building, strangers who count him. The
same night Rami's new life starts on the grill, the night Michael once lent
him and got back `Mu ndif`. Do the two nights touch? Rami decides.

## Bridge (optional, ≤200 words)

Holy Week in a city done with Easter. Yara's own week by message (the
Beirut office, her mother), not an answer to `B7ebbik`. He doesn't chase.

## Scenes

**S01 — Holy Friday, Poble-sec.** The parish has the church after the
hosts' mass, until a fixed hour. Abouna Elias has taught chemistry all day
(an ordinary school day here) and runs the evening on the clock. The bier is
dressed in flowers; Em Nicola commandeers Michael's younger hands. Give Em Nicola something
of her own this week (her daughter, the family's next capital, her hands),
delivered as a verdict. Turn: someone asks what nobody in Barcelona has
asked him, whom he breaks the fast with. The answer is the boot: no hall,
the church goes back to its hosts, so the parish eats on the pavement.

**S02 — Saturday night into Pascha.** Lights out; one flame passed hand to
hand; the procession steps into a Saturday-night street nobody warned (bar
crowds, a sea wind at the candles, balconies). `El Masi7 2am!` arrives
because the clock and the crowd force it, not as a speech. Then the fast
breaks at Abouna's open car boot: Núria (thirty years feeding a fasting
priest; `Kol shi 2abel ma tsou2`, 011) brings what she brings; Em Nicola's
red eggs; an egg-tapping duel; Michael, for once, not refusing food.

Rami's first night ends around two, half an hour's walk away in el Born.
What he does is his own act. Live options: he comes, still smelling of the
grill, for reasons of his own (hunger, the walk, something his hands made);
he sends something; he doesn't come and something of his waits at the flat;
or better. No hug, apology, thanks speech or 012 replay. If they talk, real
words: practical, sideways, funny (the grill, Quim, the first night).
Open threads: the yellow cheese Michael deferred to `Ba3d el 3id el kbir`
(011-S02); who eats the other half of the loaves.

## Whose table

The parish's: Abouna's car boot on the pavement, stocked by Núria and Em
Nicola. The community that lives in a boot (011) feeds from it. Not a feast
with a fasting guest (011-S03): everyone breaks the fast at once. And Rami
stays free: whatever he does, he isn't hosting a reconciliation.

## What changes

Michael breaks his fast inside a parish that counts him: a concrete sign he
is expected next Sunday by name (a task, a place, the second pair of hands
Abouna lacked). Rami's first full-time night is done, and something passes
between them beyond names, or visibly doesn't, by Rami's choice. `B7ebbik`
stays where 013 left it.
