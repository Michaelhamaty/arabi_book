## Intent

In February Michael found one way Sami's list shows a stale value, doubted a
second (`men el zerr bel saf7a walla men fo2?`), promised a clean run alone
and never did it. Sami shipped. On Michael's fourth day behind Walid's bar it
comes due: finishing something properly in the wrong place, while others carry
the cost. Then June gives back: a rival's pavement, a 3:00 kitchen.

## Scenes

**S01 — Wara el bar.** Sami, midnight in SF, wrecked and joking: his first big
customer, a Bay Area caterer (fictional, unnamed), sent Thursday's party food
to the old address: the `3enwan` was changed, the driver's list kept the old
one. The owner may go back to paper; Saturday is a wedding. Sami can't
reproduce it; he told them a man in Barcelona tested it; he accuses no one.
Walid's terms make it physical: a laptop on the bar is a wall; Michael ends up
on a crate under the counter, Sami lit from below, Walid stepping over his
legs. An order for Abu Nabil pings by the nail; Michael says `5ams da2aye2`.
Walid's turn, his own want inside it (an old friend laid up four floors up):
he covers the coffee and climbs to Abu Nabil himself (offstage), leaving
Michael alone behind the bar; he finds he loves it. Between coffees he does
the run alone, Sami muted, steps on the backs of order slips hung on the nail.
`Men fo2` reproduces it. Sami refuses Michael's pride: he heard "tested"
because he wanted to; his company, his call to the owner at seven. Walid
returns owing the next coffee by Abu Nabil's law. The customer's answer stays
open.

**S02 — Travessera.** An evening order carried on foot past Dalia's idling
riders. Dalia is amused by Walid's man on foot and interested in the man. Her
want: Sant Joan eve (Wed Jun 23) is her big night and she needs people who
talk to strangers, paid. Reciprocal heat, apron on, someone else's coffee
cooling; a small touch is welcome. His answer is his; a yes that loads 018 is
fine.

**S03 — Tlate el fajr.** Michael wakes on Pilar's clock. Rami, back from
service, eats from the blue tub; Inés works at the table. Rami's want: his
mother still tells el Sha8our he "helps out" in a restaurant; he wants a real
photograph of himself at Quim's grill for her, and asks Inés. She names a
price aloud, her first since 013's 8 euro, and it holds against onions and two
years of m7ammara. Then California: Maya calls, Andrew joins. Their fight is
Father's Day: Andrew has a plan, Maya wants tea and nothing else; underneath,
she is now the only little sibling for Andrew to manage, and she is done. His
vote settles nothing; they get his day's real version in a line. Andrew, who
has had Rami's number since January, asks for Rami.

**Ending — a new kind: polyphonic.** The phone on speaker on the kitchen
table; Andrew, Maya, Rami and Inés talking over each other across nine hours;
Michael, for once, neither holding it nor talking. No single last line,
verdict, message, door, stairs or still object.

## What changes

The clean run exists, late and right; Sami owns the failure. Walid has left
his bar and owes a coffee; Michael has run it alone and loves it. Dalia has
asked for Sant Joan eve. Inés has priced her work aloud; Rami wants his mother
to see what he does. Michael's two households have heard each other.
