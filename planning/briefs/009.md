# Brief 009 — Sauce and Sunrise

- Arabizi title (writer may improve): **Bala kartoun**. Predecessor: 008,
  provisionally accepted, `e827720f…cf870`.
- Dates (weekdays verified): **Wed Mar 3** night; **Fri Mar 5** after
  service into **Sat Mar 6** dawn (CET; sunrise about 7:20).
- Places: the flat in Gràcia; Rami's restaurant in el Born (fictional,
  unnamed); **Barceloneta** beach and boardwalk; Rebecca Horn's stacked rusted
  cubes (*L'Estel Ferit*); the sail-shaped hotel at the beach's end
  (described, never named); the **L4**.
- Cast: Michael 21, Rami 23, Inés 24, Yara 22 (flirtation: Yara and Michael
  only); Nadine 42 by message. New: **Quim** (54, Catalan chef-owner; plain
  Arabic; four-word commands, inventive swearing, the tasting spoon in his
  apron; a tyrant in service, tender after it; calls Rami `el Shami`).
  **Montse** (73, born in Barceloneta, a fisherman's daughter, forty years
  at a fish counter; swims every morning all year with a few old regulars;
  loud, nosy, bossy, warm; judges people by whether they'll get in the water).

## Intent

Appetite and joy. Michael's yes-habit runs on pleasure and carries him,
unslept, from Rami's kitchen to Yara's sunrise. Rami gets his own chapter:
food, chef, pride, and a secret. Yara gets a real first date, working, funny
and a little exposed, on a beach that is visibly Barcelona in March.

## Scenes

**S01 — Wed, the flat.** Rami pays his debt with "something you don't know
the name of": calçots, in season, charred and wrapped in newspaper, eaten
with hands and bibs, plus the restaurant's famous sauce, which is his
mother's mu7ammara. Quim put it on the menu as the house sauce without
Rami's name, and it sells. Rami steals by a code (only what his own hands
made) and refuses a cent toward the wine he overpaid for. Inés arrives
hungry from a job, eats like an expert, knows at once whose sauce it is, and
states her rule: don't tell her secrets, she won't keep them. Then she goes
out. Nadine's message lands mid-meal (the bakery expects
him next week; buy bread there first as a customer; papers still to check).
"Paid" makes Rami ask how much and do the maths in onions. Late, on the
balcony: Quim has offered Rami a full-time post from April, which means
leaving his unfinished degree; his family thinks the kitchen is temporary;
he has told no one, Inés included, and owes Quim an answer before Easter.
Michael promises to keep it (`akid` is available; nobody remarks on it).
Rami changes the subject by inviting him to Friday's after-service staff
meal. Yes, gladly.

**S02 (short) — Fri night, the kitchen in el Born.** Steel, steam, the crew
eating standing. Quim treats Rami with a respect Rami never described.
Michael loves all of it and never goes home. Near dawn Quim pushes a
cardboard box of leftovers at him "for whoever you're seeing at this hour."
He walks through the old streets to the sea. (May fold into S03's opening.)

**S03 — Sat, Barceloneta.** The famous latecomer is first, holding a box.
Montse comes out of the March sea, takes him for the swimmers' breakfast
delivery and shares the box out before he can explain. Yara arrives early
too, having padded his time for his lateness, and finds him feeding her
regulars. Montse knows her as the girl who swears at the sun and asks,
sideways, whether this is the one. Yara's task: first raking light through
the cubes' windows onto the sand, for her portfolio; her third attempt, and
a cloud bank is eating the sunrise. Her impatience is the comedy; her eye is
the romance. Wit both ways, real attraction. One honest thing each: hers
about home (Beirut's sea behind walls and entrance fees, against an old
woman swimming here for free); his about living "over there" one day, and
whether that dream is made of his teta's kitchen. Montse dares him into the
water. The sun breaks while he is in it, and Yara must choose what to shoot.
Montse expects him next Saturday; the light may need Yara back too.
A kiss is the writer's call, brief and mutual.

## What changes

Rami trusts Michael with a secret Inés refused to hold. Michael and Yara
have had a first date and a reason for another Saturday, offered by the sea,
not a plan. End on dialogue or movement.
