## Intent

Michael has work he loves and may not do it. For a week the papers make his
yes useless: someone needs hands and he can't give his. Pride and money in
three businesses: a baker who won't ask for help, Nadine who won't take free
work, Walid facing the future his partner wants. And pleasure: bread before
dawn, strangers, a party, flirtation for fun.

## Scenes

**S01 — Mon, the forn.** Buy bread, watch the door. He's a hopeless
anonymous customer: forty minutes, the queue talking. The door shows the
truth: good loaves gone by eight; people open it, see the line and the empty
rack, leave. Pilar knows he's Nadine's. Her terms: no questions across a
counter; from tomorrow he stands behind it. He wants to, and says he must
ask first (nobody remarks). Opposite, Arnau, chalking prices, says
why he left: six years, never allowed to touch the dough; "tell her if you
want." Michael calls Nadine by the market: papers still under review; no
unpaid work, not one hour, for her or for Pilar. Her reason comes sideways:
her mother took orders from the window above the sweets shop for thirty
years and nobody paid her, because it was family. She doesn't move. He
doesn't go back on Tuesday.

**S02 — Thu, Gràcia.** A modern Lebanese place opens a few streets from 3al
Zawiye: codes on tables, a screen, riders at the side door, a door list;
everything Marwan wants. Walid won't go alone or empty-handed (a green box,
to a restaurant). Dalia runs the night; the invitation is gratitude, and it
stings him. Between a late chef and a neighbor furious about noise, she
flirts openly; Michael enjoys flirting back. She cuts him off when he
explains the bakery and leaves mid-sentence for someone more useful. Telling
her about Saturday's sea, he leaves Yara out, and notices later. Walid
wanted the place empty or bad; it is full and the food ordinary, which is
worse. He goes quiet.

**S03 — Fri, before opening.** Michael, rested, comes as a customer,
his side of the glass, while Pilar works. He says plainly why he never
took the counter, and repeats what Arnau said. She's furious. Then the turn
is hers and costs her pride (she calls Arnau, or lets other hands into the
dough; writer's call). He buys coca for tomorrow's swimmers: Pilar's bread,
not Quim's food; why he avoids Quim stays unspoken. A message with Yara,
who learns for the first time that he has a job; what they are stays
unsaid. End on Pilar's action or the door opening to the street.

## What changes

Pilar admits in deed that she needs hands; Nadine's study has its first
owner on her terms. Papers still pending, Michael has felt what "not yet"
costs. Walid has seen Marwan's argument full on a Thursday.
Michael caught himself leaving Yara out. Carrying words worked, which
matters in 012.
