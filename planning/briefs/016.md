## Intent

Marwan has gone to Nadine without Michael, and paid for the "second
window". Michael is offered a job in his field (half an answer to Karim's
"what would you *do* there?") inside the café he loves as a guest, and
taking it costs him that. The same fortnight Pilar ends his dawns. He
crosses the counter twice: out of one shop, into another.

## Scenes

**S01 — The last dawn.** Since Nadine's money ended he has kept coming some
mornings, unpaid and happy. Pilar has counted them. She pays him from her
till, exact, and ends it: Arnau is full time, she can't pay three, and
nobody works in her shop for nothing but her. Arnau raises the shutter. At
7:00 Nadine brings the study's finding Pilar joined for (her way works);
Pilar decides who keeps it, a hint of the forn's future. From the customer
side Pilar asks Michael a regular's follow-up. Over coffee in the market
Nadine is plain: Marwan is paying for a month's pilot, orders and delivery;
she wants Michael, paid, Jun 1 – Jul 15. Marwan wants him because Walid
trusts him, and so does she: one reason, not two. She promised Walid in
February never to say he agreed; if Walid shuts the door, she doesn't come.
Michael doesn't say yes on the spot. He'll answer by Friday the 28th, after
telling Walid himself.

**S02 — The coffee that walks.** Walid knows (Marwan told him straight,
offstage) and says nothing, serving Michael exactly as before, which is
worse. The corner has been empty three days. At noon the phone rings while
Walid's hands are in dough, his 008 sentence happening (no one-sided call).
Walid can't leave; he asks without asking (wrapped man2oushe, covered
coffee, an address). Up four floors: Abu Nabil, knee on a stool, playing
both sides of a board. His correction this time is the café's own story:
when it nearly closed, Marwan's money kept it open, and Walid has never
forgiven him. He wants his coffee brought up and nothing to change, both at
once. He wins, invents a law, makes Michael pay.

**S03 — Before opening.** Michael still wakes at 3:00 on Pilar's clock.
Walid alone, kneading, chairs on the tables, nobody to perform for. Michael
says what he wants as his own want: not Marwan's case, not an apology.
Marwan lets himself in and misreads it, crediting Michael with bringing
Walid round, which costs Michael in front of Walid. The partners say the
money aloud, one grievance each: Walid's pride at being saved; Marwan's
wish for the place behind the bar Walid never offered him. Walid answers
Marwan, not Michael: yes, a month, his terms, and the terms include
Michael. Michael refuses to be only a clause and gives his own reason.

**Close — Tue Jun 1, noon.** A short passage: the first day, Michael on the
bar side, the cost in one clear sign (the window chair no longer his, Abu
Nabil not playing the staff, or a price; pick one). End on work in motion:
not a still object, quoted message, phone call or someone's verdict line.

## What changes

Pilar's dawns are closed and paid; Michael is her customer again. He has
taken Nadine's pilot on Marwan's money after telling Walid first. Walid
allowed it on his terms; the debt under the partnership is spoken; the war
has a one-month truce with Michael as its hinge. He is no longer the café's
guest. May 15 and 17 passed and he asked nobody.
