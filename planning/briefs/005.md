## 005-S02 — The train and a decision, Monday

Proposed Arabizi scene title: **Msakkar mish ma7loul**.

**Shared circumstance:** Michael boards an unnamed train toward UAB on
Monday and ends up beside Karim, who is working on a **fictional job-application
exercise**. A small practical interruption brings them into contact: papers
slip, Karim mutters over a crossed-out response, or Michael needs to move so
he can recover a page. Choose one, keep it brief, and let the interest in the
work earn the conversation. Karim has a reason to share **his own sample
exercise**, not a stranger reading confidential client information over his
shoulder. They discover their shared UAB destination naturally. No arrival
questionnaire, campus tour, missed train, ticket tutorial, or invented official
procedure is needed.

**What each wants:**

- Michael wants a usable campus day and is interested in a concrete systems
  problem. He initially sees the value of a simple measurable target: it can
  make a process less arbitrary, show where work backs up, and reveal whether
  someone actually did it. His position needs substance, not a straw man for
  Karim to knock down.
- Karim wants to send a credible application for a better work opportunity
  while retaining a view he can defend. He studies and works in Barcelona;
  current employer, subject, wages, legal status, family history, and exact
  financial need remain unspecified. He also wants company on a boring
  journey and enjoys a sparring partner who answers back.

**Practical/intellectual stake:** The exercise asks how a small support team
might handle more requests; its suggested measure rewards closing requests
quickly. Use **one invented sample request and a reopen/follow-up** to keep the
argument concrete. Karim objects that a closed record can conceal an unresolved
person and push unpaid waiting or repeated explanation onto the customer or
worker. Michael questions whether an alternative with no countable result
would let the person in charge do whatever they like. Karim cannot merely
condemn the exercise: he wants the opportunity and will submit something.

Keep all claims about this invented exercise and these characters' opinions.
No real employer is named; no assertions about actual UAB recruiting,
Spanish employment law, or 2027 AI/news are required. This is an argument about
power and incentives in a practical choice, **not** a lecture assigned to
Karim's nationality. The job is a fictional possibility, not an offered job,
a paid assignment, or an established career history.

**Complication and movement:** Let Karim's first sharp position become harder
to hold when Michael asks what he will actually send. Karim admits he does not
intend to throw the opportunity away. Michael has to distinguish acknowledging
a request from resolving it, rather than win through a clever total. They may
laugh, interrupt, misunderstand a term, and correct one another. Karim can be
stubborn and unfair for a moment; Michael can irritate him and still be useful.
Allow ordinary conversation and some personal curiosity between the working
beats. Neither knows about Sami, Yara, the dinner, or the café unless Michael
actually chooses to tell him; there is no need to retell those scenes.

**Turn and stopping point:** They stay together into a generic campus space
because Karim wants to finish the response before sending it, not because a
narrator decrees instant friendship. Karim **chooses and submits his own
answer**: retain a countable measure but make unresolved/reopened requests
visible rather than reward hiding them. The writer chooses the exact practical
form. Michael contributes a concrete objection or example; Karim retains the

see each other again; no joint venture, permanent study partnership, scheduled
weekly ritual, or new unpaid deliverable for Michael is needed. End on a lived
interaction or the send itself, not a moral aphorism.
