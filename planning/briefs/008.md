## Intent

Michael keeps this deadline. Let the habit be dangerous because it
**works**: competence earns the next, bigger yes.

1. **The interview (S01).** Walid turns one real order into theatre: a
   phone change scribbled in red on the same paper on the nail, the only
   person near the phone being Walid with his hands in dough. Abu Nabil
   heckles from his corner with a different version. Nadine laughs, and
   shows a human side (her parents' shop in Damascus). Michael almost
   proposes a fix and doesn't. Walid stops at exactly twenty minutes. Nadine
   decides her colleague's week on Tuesday evening.
2. **Two copies (S02–S03).** Inés edits his page hard and makes him help with
   her unpaid client. The page is emailed by lunchtime. It is printed on
   Inés's glossy photo paper, the only paper in the flat. Michael and Rami
   drop Walid's copy at the café on their way to collect Rami's shelf. Walid
   reads his own sentence and goes quiet. He names his partner **Marwan**
   (wants apps and delivery) and files the page in his private book. The
   shelf carry through Gràcia is rough, funny and profane.
3. **The yeses (S03 end).** Inés's client pays; wine on the tiny balcony.
   Nadine offers paid work (8 h/week, six weeks, three other businesses,
   pending his paperwork) and says "think and reply". He doesn't think. Yara
   invites him to photograph the dawn light at Barceloneta on Saturday at
   7:30: `Ana jey`. Inés guesses whose beach.

## What changes

Marwan enters the café story. Inés's client pays. Michael now has paid work,
the first date-like invitation from Yara, and Rami's dinner tomorrow, all
accepted in one evening.
