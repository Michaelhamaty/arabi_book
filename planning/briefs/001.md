## Purpose and shape

One compact departure chapter. Use two short friendship snapshots, a small visit
with Hassan, the family gathering, and the drive/drop-off with Zeina. Give most
space to the family and mother-son interaction. The first three units can be
very brief; five scene IDs do not mean five equally long set pieces.

Michael should be recognizable through play, warmth, curiosity, and composure.
Plant overcommitment lightly—perhaps accepting several requests to call—without
manufacturing a failure before the story has begun. No required lesson or crisis.

- End on an ordinary gesture or unfinished exchange, not a farewell monologue.
- No romance, women-as-prospects, dating jokes, or former romantic interests.
