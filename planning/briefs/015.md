## Intent

Karim's answer comes back: an insult that is also a job. Michael says out
loud the dream he has never had to defend, living in Lebanon or Syria one
day, to the one friend for whom "home" is an offer on the table. A real
fight, blows landing both ways.

## Scenes

**S01 — The answer (train).** In 005 Karim wanted someone beside him when
the reply came. It came at dawn; he kept it unopened for Michael. The
Barcelona post is filled; the company offers him the same role in its
**Amman office** from September, on local terms (a fraction of the pay, his
claim), "closer to home." Karim reads it in his corporate-sarcastic voice;
they closed his request and opened a new one in Amman. Michael, delighted,
sells Amman to him: his own dream in someone else's life. Karim shuts it with one line. Opposite sits Salma, a
two-metre survey pole between her knees and labelled bags of wet sand, on
her way to the lab from a dawn on the beach. Measured with the stranger,
brief when asked, forceful when Michael romanticizes Barceloneta. One dry
line from her about Amman cuts deeper than his enthusiasm. Karim: tonight,
her roof, `el 2ahwe 3layye`. She allows it on a condition of her own.

**S02 — The roof (argileh).** Her argileh, his coffee (005's promise paid
on the day he may have no job), dusk into night. Salma wants her coals
lit and men to stop explaining coasts.
Staying versus leaving, money versus principle, what each owes a homeland:
Karim calls Michael's Lebanon a summer with a return ticket and asks what he
would *do* there. Michael has no answer, then lands his own blow: Karim
romanticizes Barcelona exactly as Michael does Beirut, and pride may cost
him a real job. Salma is from el Mezze, a city with no sea, studying a sea.
Barcelona trucks sand **in** to keep beaches the storms take; as a girl in
Tartus she watched trucks take sand **out** to build. Nobody explains it.
Her position (going back, not, or neither) is hers and sounds like neither
man. She names Michael's charm for what it is. Karim acts on his own terms:
accept, refuse, or answer in the company's language (home, at the Barcelona
price) and risk losing it. Michael doesn't press the button for him.

## What changes

Karim has answered and can't take it back. Michael's dream has met its
first hard question, carried into July unanswered. The friendship is real:
a fight, a roof, a paid coffee. Salma exists in her own right; any spark is
small and two-sided (she asks him one real question, or asks him to hold
the pole some dawn; or neither).
