## Intent

Two calendars run through every life: the city feasts while Michael's fast
begins; Rami needs time; Inés goes home for an Easter she claims not to
want. Michael gets something just for himself, a parish, and a priest too
busy to be his guru. He lends his Easter to one friend and keeps a silence
for another.

## Scenes

**Bridge.** A paragraph: Montse's verdict on the coca; Clean Monday; Nadine
writes that `el wra2` is ready; first paid dawns behind Pilar's glass.

**S01 — Palm Sunday, the borrowed church.** The hosts' palms fill the
morning; the Arabic liturgy runs late. Outside, children with palms and
white braided palmons; inside, grandmothers carrying icons round the same
building (the Sunday of Orthodoxy). Michael's pleasure: chant in his own
language, the back row, strangers asking where he's from. Abouna
Elias wants the hall cleared and to reach Núria's family lunch, where he
is the fasting guest every year. He conscripts Michael's arms; carrying,
they argue for fun (perhaps Easter's date as an algorithm older than
computers). Michael leaves with a parish, not a lesson.

**S02 — Thursday, 3:15, the kitchen.** Three clocks cross: Rami in from
Quim's Holy Week crush, Michael up for Pilar's oven, Inés packed for a train
south: home for Semana Santa, first time in two years, because a client
pays her to photograph it, so she needn't admit she wants to go. Rami wants
time. Quim said "before el Fes7", never whose; he borrows Michael's, May 2.
Michael's yes is delighted; his true date becomes Rami's alibi. Inés
hears only a calendar joke. Yara by message, here or in S03: the
upper-window light. Perhaps Michael says no for once (Saturday is mona
day) and she gets the window without him.

**S03 — Easter Sunday, Walid's flat.** Michael brings a mona from Pilar's
(empty hands are shameful), eggs he won't eat. Walid cooks; his mother
corrects him. Odette keeps loading lamb on the fasting guest's plate;
the ma3moul is butter. How strict he is stays light and his own.
Marwan argues warmly, with numbers; Odette agrees. He learns what
Michael's company makes and lights up. Dalia has invited him. Michael holds
Walid's `Ba3d` under friendly pressure (does Marwan already know? writer's
call). Quim's reply to Rami may arrive. End on someone else's choice at
the table, or a laugh mid-act.

## What changes

Michael has a parish and paid hours at Pilar's. Rami's deadline moves to
May 2 with his help; the secret is heavier and still theirs. Marwan
is on the page with a want that runs through Michael (Nadine's company);
the partnership argument is spoken before Walid's mother. Michael's
silence for Walid costs him something.
