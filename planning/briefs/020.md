## Intent

Lebanon begins the morning after, when the generator's rest hour stops the
fan. Michael's `3ish, mu zour` gets its first real day: a host's Saturday, not
a program. The man he was sent to has a way out. The pleasure is real (heat,
the sea at dusk, a woman who prices everything and laughs), and so is the
bill. By night the dream still stands, smaller and more exact.

## Scenes

**S01 — Six o'clock, the fan.** Michael wakes soaked in Wassim's room. Tarek
has hosted since yesterday: his mother's Chouf food in a fridge that warms
when the power goes, the family group asking if the guest ate, and on the
fridge the Program (Jbeil, Jeita, the Cedars) he has driven for every
cousin from abroad. He performs it as a guide, funny and worn out. The site
confirms tonight's concrete pour; Tarek, between honour and work, apologizes
theatrically. The turn is Michael's pleasure, not politeness: he wants
Tarek's Saturday. Tarek can't tell if he's insulted.

**S02 — The pour.** Dusk on the top slab: mixers, the pump's arm, a Syrian
crew (Abu 5aled may tease Michael's Damascene; no pity, no speech). Below, the
Corniche: fishermen, men jumping off the rocks. These flats will sell that
sea by the square metre, at a price Tarek will never pay. He wants a
witness, not advice: he tells Michael about Riyadh, the first person told. Michael says his dream; Tarek, who lived its
reverse, answers with something specific, not Karim's question. Michael's
urge to decide for him is the complication. The turn: Tarek asks him to
tell no one, Rami included, and Michael keeps a secret instead of giving an
opinion.

**S03 — Midnight, the moteur.** The state's hours end; the breaker drops
(AC, water heater, two phones). Tarek is asleep on his feet, so Michael goes
down: a generator behind a shuttered shop, roar and diesel, and Nayla (25,
said on the page), whose father owns it. Summers she runs four streets of
subscriptions; the rest of the year she teaches maths. She knows every flat
by floor and amps, and lets slip that Tarek went from five to ten on
Thursday "for the American", and of course said nothing. She flirts by
arithmetic, pricing his dream line by line; he flirts back with curiosity
about a business built on the state's absence. She is staying for good.
Reciprocal, light, at most a touch; she finishes her round, doesn't wait.

**Ending.** Upstairs, Tarek falls asleep mid-sentence and it stays
unfinished; the guest does one small piece of the host's work, choosing what
the flat's amps are for. Not transit talk, a bell, a table, a wall, a
withheld gesture, a walk, a message, stairs, a speakerphone, a dance, water,
or a still object.

## What changes

Michael is off the program and holds Tarek's Riyadh secret, from Rami too;
Tarek has said it aloud for the first time. Michael has seen what being
hosted costs and carries a piece of it his own way. He knows Nayla. The
dream has a bill and a breaker in it and still stands.
