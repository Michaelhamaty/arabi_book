## Intent

The last chapter of Barcelona I. Everyone Michael said yes to has a claim on
his last fortnight. The chapter leaves things open on purpose and says which: a verdict that settles the screen but
not the partners, a game kept instead of finished, a yes with limits he names,
and Yara, offstage since April, deciding what the summer is. Joy first:
Michael a customer again in the window chair, the city at its hottest, a
night sea nobody guards.

## Scenes

**Bridge, in passing.** A paragraph: Sant Joan noon on two hours' sleep;
the coffee went up four floors at 12:00, paid. Friday the
board was finished before Em Jamil (result and her line are the writer's).
Exams went past in the heat: generic data/CS life (the FGC to Bellaterra, a
group project). No replay of 018.

**S01 — Saturday, seven.** The month is over, and Marwan lets himself in at
7:00 in his apron anyway: four Saturdays have become his. Michael, a
customer again, has the window chair and his write-up open.
Nadine brings what the month showed, told as people: who ordered (Nabil for
his father, a few who can't come down, not many more), what it cost, who
carried. The surprise may be that Marwan's own numbers argue against his
screen, and that what worked was a second pair of hands, his, offered across
the bar. Walid hears money in everything Marwan offers and has never thanked
him. The screen's verdict is clear by the end; the partnership keeps a new
edge for Barcelona II. Michael is witness, not fixer: he doesn't offer them
his September. Nadine turns to him with paid hours that continue (from
Lebanon, or from September); his yes is exact, with limits he names, not
`akid`. The barber's lost order only if it earns something new.

**S02 — The last game.** Thursday afternoon, the corner. Abu Nabil and
Michael start a game that can't finish before he goes. No law this time;
Abu Nabil says what he wants plainly or not at all, and Michael stays
`Amerkani`. Walid can't block his board for two months, so the position is
kept somewhere that lasts (in red on Michael's page in the brown book,
say). No speeches. Short. Somewhere brief, Sami's caterer, kept or lost, in
Sami's words. Rami's grill photo and his mother's answer only as a passing
beat, if earned.

**S03 — A sea with no doorman.** Yara chooses the night: a Poblenou beach
after dark, warm water. In Beirut the sea is behind walls and tickets; she
wants one swim in this one first, her want. He wants her in Beirut in
August; she won't be the guide through whom he loves it. Since May 15 he
has never asked what she told the office. The turn: he asks, and she tells
what she chooses; if she tells, make it clear, later chapters will hold it.
Anything about the summer, exclusivity included, is said aloud or doesn't
exist. Dalia's kiss is not this scene's subject unless Yara asks; then he
doesn't lie.

**Ending — under the water.** The last beat is physical and wordless inside
the night sea: a held breath, the city's noise gone at once, a hand in the
dark. Not dialogue, not motion that keeps going (018), not a still object, no
packing or airport.

## What changes

The pilot is judged; the screen's fate is set and the partners stand
differently. Michael has made one exact, paid yes and one plain "I can't"
(Walid's unasked September, Dalia's `la 7ada`, or a term of Yara's). The game
waits for September. Michael and Yara have asked and answered what they kept
walking around, and August has terms.
