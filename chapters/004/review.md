### Language doubts for the reviewer

- `dabsh` used for the double (5-5), per the calibration palette. The 6-5
  roll is `shesh besh` and 1-1 is `habbyek`. Check that `dabsh` is the right
  Damascene name and spelling.
- `Tezz` (Abu Nabil, contemptuous) and `Ya ebn el kalb` (to the dice): intended
  as earthy old-man profanity. Is the severity right in a full café?
- `Mashalla 3al makina` as a deliberate jinx (evil-eye logic): does it read
  naturally as spite from Abu Nabil? Spelled `Mashalla` (no lexicon entry).
- Homs joke: "El yom jem3a, mu arba3a. Ya3ni hiye 3a2le." This relies on the
  Wednesday Homs jokes. Check that it lands without explanation and isn't
  over-familiar for a public jab at a woman of her age.
- Em Jamil `Allah ysam7o` (God forgive him, for teaching you): check the idiom.
- Walid: `Hayde mesh menne ilak`, `bkassrlak yeha`, `El rbe7 2imto 3alye`,
  `metl wa7ad nater natije`, `bala tarbaye`. Check the Lebanese forms.

  purpose against Walid's `Hayda`.
- Camille `Ili ana?` ("for me?"), `b sa55ena` (I'm heating it), and `Hada mu
