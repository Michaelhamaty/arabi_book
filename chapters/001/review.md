Texture/voice notes:

- S01 (Sami) and S04 (the family) are lively and have specific bits: the wrong-cup
  swap, the falafel-shop fantasy, the passport-face joke, "Barcelona mu Moscow,"
  three days that become two. Sami's catastrophizing and self-insult read as his own.
- S02: Fares is now consistently Lebanese (`mesh`, `eza`, `hayda`, `e7ke`,
  `menne`, `ba2a`, `ba3da`). His teasing rhythm differs from Sami's. The
  "marrer! marrer!" exchange is the best friend-insult beat in the chapter.
- S03 is deliberately sparse. That suits Hassan's card (few words, tea, teasing
  without smiling, one exact question). It is not bloodless. `"Wallah" ma3ak
  sahle ktir` and the receipt line carry it.
- Widad is not overdone. If anything she is under-blessed: only `ya t2berni`
  plus her sly `Ana ma talabet shi`. A writer could give her one short blessing
  when Michael stands up (e.g. `Allah y5allik la 2elna`). Not applied, because
  her scene works.
- The profanity range is mild and in character (`ya 7mar`, `ya 7ayawan`,
  `tfeh`, `ya 2abaday`). It fits loving family and friend teasing, and none of
  it was sanitized.
- Michael stays Damascene throughout (`mu`, `iza`, `lissa`, `halla2`, `2ili`).

Uncertain expressions left in place:

- `jacket` (S01, S03, S05): this is an English spelling in narration. Chapters 002–003
  use the same word, so it was left alone to avoid cross-chapter drift. A
  batch decision is needed on `jakeit`/`jakét` versus keeping it as an accepted loan.
- `tester` (S01 Sami): a tech loan in dialogue. It is plausible for an SF founder, but it is English.
- `ra2em el sakan` (S04): the intended meaning, "the housing contact's number," is
  slightly vague.
- `Mrattabin kell shi` (S03): the participle-plural reading is plausible.
  `Kell shi mrattab` would be the safer alternative.
- `2abel ma tebrod` (S01, of coffee): both `tebrod` and `tebrad` occur.
- `3arabayet walad` (stroller), `msabbe` (cursing, Fares), `Iza tla3 na2esni
  shi` (S03): these read as natural, but they are model-proposed.
- `Allah ysahhel 3leik` (S05, new): this is a common travel/success blessing. It is
  model-proposed and not verified.
