his four Saturdays behind the bar sold thirty per cent more than May's.
Nadine: `ma baddak barnamej, baddak idein` — `W el idein?` — `Idak.` The
screen comes down; Nabil phones the landline. Marwan offers a free noon hour

`wen sha3r idak`. Abu Nabil sets up the board. Sami writes: the caterer stayed

brown book; on the back of Michael's glossy March page Walid writes the

7ajar lal 3alam. El zahr ma3i.` And: `Rou7, Amerkani. W la terja3 Lebnani.
