## Summary (spoilers)

**S01, El jorn.**
- **Arrival.** On the drive up, Michael crosses himself privately at a roadside church; it is Sunday. They hear the house before they see it: Em Tarek, about 56, has been pounding kebbe nayye in the stone `jorn` since five, though the tray was finished three hours earlier.
- **Abu Tarek.** He is about 61, a retired stonemason who built the house by hand. He says almost nothing. Asked if he built it: `Ana. W 7mar.`
- **Tarek sidelined.** Em Tarek seats her son in the guest chair in the sun and beats him to every task. His one allowed job is the photo for `El 3ayle`: `Michael byoq3od w byakol. Enta btoq3od w btsawwer.`
- **The fourth piece.** Her right hand cramps open and shut. Michael is about to refuse a fourth piece, sees her hand, says `Tislam idik` (`W idak.`), and eats it.
- **Abu Tarek's one question** is about the rusting rebar on the roof. Tarek: if nobody cleans it and pours, two years, then it's good for nothing. `Sentein.`
- **Em Tarek's meal.** She eats last, standing at the kitchen door, from the tray's edges, and says `Akalt` when she hasn't.
- **The roof.** She sends Tarek off to his uncle's with two trays and takes Michael up to turn the drying za3tar: `B kaffak, 3a mahlak. Eza 3asarto, byetfarfat.`
  - She asks whether he ate, then what Tarek told him to tell her. Since boyhood, Tarek has taught guests what to say.
  - Michael finishes Tarek's unfinished sentence from 020 with something true that is not the secret: `Ta3ban. Mu za3lan.` She answers: `Ta3ban ba3rfo. Men tlat snin ta3ban.`
  - Her evidence: on Thursday Tarek asked his father for his original degree certificate, as Wassim did a month before Dubai.
  - She asks nothing he would have to refuse (`Enta dayf. El dayf ma mn7ammlo shi.`). She wants it from her son, at her own table. She binds Michael not to tell Tarek she asked: `Qello sa2altak eza akalt. W hayda sa7.`
  - He came up the roof with one secret and goes down with two.
- **The drive down.** Tarek asks what she wanted. `Sa2aletni iza akalt.` Tarek laughs and slaps the wheel: `Sett w 3eshrin sene nafs el so2al.`

**Bridge.**
- Em Tarek adds Michael to `El 3ayle`, which now has thirty members, and the question now comes to him. He answers with photos, always of Tarek eating, and writes `Mni7. 3am yeshte8el.` thirty times.
- Tarek works night pours. At two in the morning he does sums over the crumpled sheet: `Nam, 5ayyo. Hayda 7sab.`

**S02, El mina.**
- **The drive.** Tarek keeps the Jbeil promise. The car has no AC. He tries the bus-guide voice once in the Jounieh traffic and gives it up: `El dalil ma byemshi 3a 5amsin daraje.`
- **Rami's call.** He rings on his split-shift break, rough and hurt. Em Tarek has phoned him three times this week, and Tarek answers his messages with photos of kebbe.
  - Michael holds: `Ma fini 2ellak.`
  - Rami swears (`Yel3an abu hal "ma fini"… ya 7ayawan!`) and throws March at him: in half an hour Michael told Inés Rami's Quim secret. `Ma3i ma kan 3andak hal sharaf. Ma3o sar 3andak?`
  - Michael has a good answer, and it is all about himself, so he doesn't give it: `Eh. Ma3ak ma kan.`
  - Rami: `5ara 3a hal sharaf. 3tini yeh.` Michael hands Tarek the phone with one word, `Rami.`
- **The fisherman.** While Tarek walks the harbour wall, Michael waits with an old fisherman (about 70, in an old Brazil shirt) who has three finger-sized fish.
  - On the fish: `El samak ra7 3a 2obros. Hniik fi kahraba.` On the bait: cheese, because `Samak Jbeil t3allam men el souwwa7.`
  - His son in Montreal always asks what he caught, and he always says `samak`: `Shu baddo ya3mel bi Montreal b "ma sedt"?`
- **Tarek returns.** He has told Rami everything himself. Rami's message for Michael: he's a donkey and still angry with him. Rami also told Tarek that his mother called three times, always Rami, never him.
- **Ending.** `El a7ad. 3al sat7. Sa2letak shi?` — `Sa2aletni iza akalt.` — `W akalt?`
