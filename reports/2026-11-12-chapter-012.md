## Summary (spoilers)

Two weeks into paid dawns, Pilar puts a blade in Michael's hand to score
thirty loaves (`El 3ajine bya3ref el id el metraddade`); the cooling bread
crackles (`El 5obez bye7ki. Ne7na la2.`), Arnau is back in a white apron,
and at seven Pilar lets Michael raise the shutter for the first time. Under
it is Yara, sleepless, holding her stair maquette for a nine o'clock jury.
Pilar asks Michael about his burned finger the way she asks Roser about the
grandson, so he has become a regular; on the pavement Yara shows him
landings wide enough for three people (`Darraj fi jiran`). Pilar's verdict:
she carries the box from underneath, like hot bread. That afternoon Inés is
back from Sevilla with nine magazine prints, her name under them, and one
she didn't send: her mother in the crowd, looking at the camera. She reads
the washed, empty m7ammara tub and Rami's afternoon sleep as Rami leaving,
and asks Michael what `2 ayyar` means. He tells her everything and adds a
plan: Rami's name on the menu, a cut per plate, part-time on the grill.
Rami walks in on it (`3am tet2ammaro 3layye?` / `Eh.`). Inés warns she
keeps no secrets: `2abel shway kan sa7bi. Halla2 sar ser.` Tuesday night at
Dalia's, Marwan watches no one stay and Walid counts the riders' bags; Dalia
finishes her sentence this time. Wednesday, with ten minutes before Quim's
grill, Rami takes Michael apart: the "study", the sauce with a price on it,
the degree as the story his mother tells el Sha8our, Michael who never asked
his own pay, his own code turned on Michael (`Hada ana 3emelto… W enta
shelto w 3tayto`). In his fury he lets slip that he cries on the metro.
Inés refuses to be Michael's excuse and confesses her own: she went to
Sevilla for her mother. Rami hands back the borrowed Easter (`Bterja3lak
yeh. Mu ndif.`), makes Michael pay half the 009 wine, presses a euro back
into his hand (`Halla2 ma 7ada ilo shi 3and 7ada`), and asks Inés to wait
up. At two in the morning they make the sauce together; through the wall
Michael hears Rami laugh from his belly, and doesn't hear the joke.
