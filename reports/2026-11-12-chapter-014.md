Michael's fast (`Lissa?`). He pins the church's address and times to the

it's a May Day march (`Ma3 3amel wa7ad. Najjar.`). At 23:59 Michael scratches
his ear and `El Masi7 2am!` is proclaimed on the pavement in front of a bar;

fast breaks at the open boot: Núria's cold lamb and fuet (`Men el Lazi2iyye
bel som. Men Sarrià bel fuet.`), Em Nicola's onion-skin eggs, and Michael's
egg ends Abouna's thirty-year streak. Then Rami heckles the duel from the

note). His first night: 220 plates, Quim silent behind him for two hours, then

ya 7mar.` Michael finally says `Mabrouk` (`… la wa7ad etjawwaz w ma 3azamni`

admits he eats the other half of the loaves (`Min fakkart? El jiran?`).
