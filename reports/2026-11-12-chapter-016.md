## Summary (spoilers)

Since Nadine's money ran out, Michael has kept getting up at three for
Pilar's dawns, unpaid and happy. On Wed May 12 Pilar opens the till before
the shift ends and pays him for all seven mornings to the cent (242.50):
Arnau is full time, she can't pay three, and in her forn only one person
works for nothing, her. `Ma bteshte8el. Btsir zboun.` He leaves by the
kitchen door and comes back through the bell to buy a brown loaf, and she
asks about Sunday's clock the way she asks regulars. Nadine's study says the
forn needs a second pair of hands, not software; Pilar hands the folder to
Arnau unread (`Iza mett, btla2ih bel dorj, ta7t el masari`). In the market
bar Nadine tells Michael that Marwan came to her three times and the third
time paid: a month's pilot of phone orders and on-foot delivery, the
"shibbek tani", with Michael paid from Jun 1 to Jul 15. Both want him for
one reason, Walid trusts him; if Walid shuts the door she won't come. He
holds his "eh" until he has told Walid himself. Marwan tells Walid first.
For two weeks Walid serves Michael exactly as before, which is worse; May 15
and 17 pass and Michael asks no one. On Tue May 25 Abu Nabil, three days
off with his knee, phones and is put on speaker (`Enta Lebnani. El Lebnani
bidabber 7alo`); Walid sends Michael up with a covered coffee and a note
without asking. Over tawle Abu Nabil tells the café's debt: closed in the
korona months, about to hand back the keys, reopened on Marwan's Dubai
money, and Walid has never said thank you (`fi zlam bysam7ouk 3a kell shi,
ella ennak 5allastoun`). He wants his coffee brought up and nothing to
change (`Hada mu delivery. Hada Walid.`), wins, and invents a law: whoever
carries the coffee up pays for the next. On Fri May 28 at 6:40, Michael
tells Walid alone that he wants the job, not to persuade or apologize.
Marwan lets himself in and credits Michael with talking Walid round. Walid
says what he hears when Marwan says "shibbek": money; Marwan says what he
wants: `Sab3 snin w ma 3tayteni tablier.` Walid's terms: one month, no
screen facing customers, delivery on foot, the paper stays on the nail,
Michael behind the bar, and Marwan behind it on Saturdays in an apron.
Michael refuses to be a clause and says why (Karim's question); asked who
he works for: `3and Nadine.` Walid throws him an apron: `Nazzel el karase.`
Tue Jun 1: a stranger has his window chair; the first order on the screen is
Nabil's, for his father (`Ma t5allih yedfa3`), and Michael takes the stairs
two at a time.
