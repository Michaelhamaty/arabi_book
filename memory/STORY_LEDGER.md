### Causal summary

In SF, Sami can't stop working. He has an engineer testing his product who
stares at him whenever he gets stuck, until Sami explains. Michael tells him to
sit behind the tester, and offers to test the build himself in his first week
away. In LA, Fares walks laps with Michael. He calls out Michael's habit of
answering talk with plans, and promises news of next week's game only if they
win. At Hassan's home, they drink tea in few words. Hassan admits he wanted to
drive Michael to the airport but Zeina said "ana," and he hands Michael his
jakeit at the door.

At Zeina's home, Maya mocks Michael's packing (two near-identical sweaters).
Andrew demands the address and Rami's number and sets a two-day silence rule.
Widad pulls Michael to sit beside her, and two timer photos are taken. On the
drive, Michael admits he is a little scared of starting from zero, and happy
too. Zeina asks for real talk instead of telegrams, and he asks for her news in
return. He promises a message on landing and a call after he rests, says
"B7ebbik, mama," kisses her hand, and walks into LAX without opening his phone.
No romance or flirtation.

### Relationship changes

None break or turn. The chapter establishes texture: Sami's founder panic,
Fares's sharp Lebanese teasing, Hassan's tea-and-one-question care, Maya's
merciless love, Andrew's deadlines, Widad's blessings, and Zeina's demand for
honest ordinary talk.

### Causal summary

Michael lands and messages Zeina. Following Rami's message ("the green door,
not the place next to it"), he finds **two** green doors: the shop next door
was painted the same green about a month ago. Rami arrives late with bread
(the bakery queue was `metl dor el jawazat`) and drops his phone. Michael takes
the bread so Rami can open the door, and they haul the big suitcase up two
floors together. Rami's kitchen shift was moved earlier, so he can't show
Michael the neighborhood today.

In the flat, Inés is laying out prints for a client who wants exactly ten
photos of an empty venue, due today. She guards the table; Rami is sent to the
kitchen with the bread. Michael gets his room and two keys on a ring, and tests
only the flat key. He makes bread and cheese for himself and Inés (no cucumber).
They agree the photo with people in it is better; she told the client `tayyeb`
anyway (`Hada esmo sho8ol`). Rami leaves eating half of Michael's bread. He
bargains with Inés for a plate's worth of table space tonight, against her
demand that he leave food in the fridge; nothing is agreed. Michael clears Inés's
plate too early and returns it. He messages Hassan that he'll sleep and then
call, and lies down with the suitcase still closed. No romance or flirtation.

### Relationship changes

- Michael and Rami: message contacts become flatmates with easy rough humor.
  Rami's help is real but limited by work.
- Michael and Inés: a working truce with jokes. She threatens him (`bedba7ak`)
  as warmth. He notices her work and agrees with her taste.
- Rami and Inés: an established two-year bickering rhythm that exists without
  Michael.

- Locations: Michael's room in the flat; neighborhood streets; 3al Zawiye.

### Causal summary

Michael wakes at 8:30 pm, showers, unpacks, and calls Hassan for a quarter hour
(narrated: the cold, the room, Rami, a prayer and half a joke). Then he calls
Zeina, who is at breakfast in LA. He tells her about Rami, Inés, and the
stairs, slightly polishing the bread story (`hek btetla3 a7la bel 2osas`), and
admits he felt like someone forgotten at the airport. He asks about her day.
She asks how Hassan sounded. They end on a reciprocal deal: she goes out for
tomatoes and bread and reports what she saw (`Hada el ettifa2`).

Next morning he tests the building key and walks without earbuds until he finds
**3al Zawiye**. Walid is on the phone scolding someone who always says "tomorrow."
Abu Nabil plays both sides of a tawle board in the corner, recruits Michael,
mocks a Shami who can't play, and beats him by mars. The espresso machine dies.
Michael offers to look up its manual, and Walid refuses (`Hayde 3omra 2a2dam
menak`). Michael says he'd love a place like this one day. Walid lists rent,
electricity, and the tomorrow-man: `El 2ahwe hiye el jez2 el tani.` Abu Nabil
asks Michael back for **Friday evening**, and Michael says `Ana jey`. Walking
home, he gets Inés's text inviting him to Camille's **Friday night** dinner,
and answers `Akid! Ana jey.` without noticing the clash. At the crossroads he
turns away from home and keeps walking. No romance or flirtation.

### Relationship changes

- Michael and Zeina: the first real Barcelona call. Silence between them is
  easy, as in the car. Their reporting deal now runs both ways.
- Michael and Walid: customer and owner. Mutual interest, dry pushback, and
  names only at the bill. No familiarity yet.
- Michael and Abu Nabil: a trash-talk rivalry with a standing invitation.
- Michael and Inés: she includes him in her social life (no obligation, she
  says).
